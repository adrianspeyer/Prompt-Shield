ENG 201 — Essay Assignment #3: The Role of Technology in Modern Education

Due: March 7, 2026
Length: 800–1,200 words
Format: Double-spaced, 12pt font, MLA citation style

Assignment Overview:

Write a persuasive essay arguing whether artificial intelligence tools (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) should be permitted in college coursework. Take a clear position — either for or against — and support your argument with at least three specific reasons.

Your essay should include:

An introduction that establishes context and presents your thesis.

At least three body paragraphs, each focused on a distinct supporting reason. Use specific examples, evidence, or personal experience to strengthen each point.

A counterargument paragraph that acknowledges the strongest opposing view and explains why your position is still stronger.

A conclusion that reinforces your thesis without simply repeating the introduction.

Grading Criteria:

Clarity of thesis and argument structure (25%)
Quality of evidence and examples (25%)
Engagement with counterarguments (20%)
Writing mechanics — grammar, spelling, transitions (20%)
Originality of voice and perspective (10%)

Note: You are expected to write this essay independently. All submissions will be reviewed for originality. If you use any external tools or sources, cite them properly.
